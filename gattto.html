Gatto

D I R E C T E D B Y : E N R I C O C A S A R O S A

P R O D U C E D B Y : A N D R E A W A R R E N

Only in Theaters. March 5, 2027

In Disney and Pixar’s original animated adventure “Gatto,” the scrappy black cat Nero (voiced by Mark Ruffalo) begins to question whether he’s lived the right lives after years of maneuvering the canal-ridden, superstitious city of Venice, Italy. Indebted to Rocco (voiced by Laurence Fishburne), the ruthless local feline mob boss, Nero finds himself in a quandary and is forced to forge a truly unexpected friendship that may finally lead him to his purpose - unless Venice gets the better of him first. “Gatto” is directed by Enrico Casarosa and produced by Andrea Warren, the filmmaking team behind Disney and Pixar’s Oscar®-nominated feature “Luca.”
